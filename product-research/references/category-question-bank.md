# Category Question Bank

Use only the relevant category section. Explain tradeoffs briefly before asking.

## Personal Care

### Shampoo and Conditioner

- Hair type: short/long, fine/thick, curly/straight, oily/dry/normal.
- Scalp issue: itch, inflammation, flakes, dandruff, seborrheic symptoms, sensitivity, fragrance reaction.
- Daily vs occasional treatment.
- Fragrance-free preference.
- Silicone preference: silicones can smooth hair but may weigh down fine hair or feel like buildup.
- Sulfate preference: SLS/SLES clean well but can irritate or dry some scalps.
- Actives: piroctone olamine, ketoconazole, selenium sulfide, salicylic acid, zinc pyrithione where available, panthenol, glycerin, niacinamide, menthol, tea tree.
- Irritants to watch: perfume/fragrance allergens, essential oils, methylisothiazolinone, methylchloroisothiazolinone.
- Hair feel priority: lightweight, volume, smoothing, fresh/cooling, fragrance.

### Deodorant and Antiperspirant

- Main job: reduce sweat, reduce smell, avoid marks, avoid irritation.
- Antiperspirant vs deodorant: aluminum salts reduce sweat; deodorants mainly reduce odor.
- Skin sensitivity, shaving irritation, eczema, fragrance sensitivity.
- Fragrance-free vs scented.
- Format: roll-on, stick, spray, cream.
- White/yellow marks tolerance.
- Actives/claims: aluminum chlorohydrate, aluminum zirconium, alcohol, baking soda, acids, zinc ricinoleate, triethyl citrate.
- Irritants to watch: perfume allergens, alcohol denat, baking soda, essential oils.

### Toothpaste

- Goal: cavity prevention, sensitivity, gum health, whitening, breath, SLS avoidance.
- Fluoride requirement: standard fluoride is usually the default for cavity prevention.
- Sensitivity actives: potassium nitrate, stannous fluoride, arginine.
- Gum/antibacterial claims: stannous fluoride, zinc salts, CPC.
- Whitening: abrasives and peroxide claims can increase sensitivity.
- SLS sensitivity: can bother some users with mouth ulcers or irritation.
- Taste and foam preference.
- Age constraints and medical/dental advice when symptoms persist.

### Sunscreen

- Use case: daily face, sports, beach, kids, sensitive skin.
- SPF and UVA protection.
- Chemical vs mineral filters: mineral can be gentler for some but heavier/whiter.
- Eye sting, acne tendency, fragrance sensitivity.
- Water resistance and reapplication practicality.
- Finish: matte, invisible, moisturizing, no white cast.

## Appliances

### Air Fryer

- Household size and portions.
- Basket vs oven style: basket is fast and easy; oven style holds more but is bulkier.
- Capacity in liters and usable basket area.
- Counter/storage space.
- Dishwasher-safe basket and tray.
- Noise, smell, preheat time, max temperature.
- Manual controls vs presets.
- Replacement parts and warranty.
- Common review failures: coating peel, basket fit, fan noise, hard cleaning.

### Vacuum

- Floor types, pets, allergies, stairs.
- Corded vs cordless: cordless is convenient; corded usually has better sustained power.
- Bagged vs bagless: bagged is cleaner for dust/allergy; bagless is cheaper to run but messier.
- Battery runtime and replaceability.
- Weight, noise, filter availability, spare parts.

## Electronics and Computers

### Computer

- Workload: browsing, office, coding, photo/video, CAD, gaming, local AI.
- OS preference and required software.
- Portability vs performance.
- Screen size, resolution, brightness, external monitor needs.
- RAM and storage needs; upgradeability.
- Ports, dock, charging, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth.
- Battery life and noise.
- Keyboard/trackpad preference.
- Repairability, warranty, expected lifespan.
- Compatibility with existing peripherals.

### Monitor

- Primary use: coding, office, design, gaming.
- Size, resolution, pixel density, ultrawide vs dual monitors.
- USB-C power delivery and hub needs.
- Refresh rate and panel type.
- Ergonomics: stand, VESA, brightness, flicker, glare.
- Mac/Windows scaling issues.

## Clothing

### T-Shirt and Clothing Brand

- Fit: slim, regular, relaxed, long/short torso, sleeve preference.
- Fabric: cotton, merino, linen, synthetic, blends.
- Climate and use: office, gym, travel, everyday.
- Durability: neck shape, shrinkage, pilling, seam twisting.
- Logo visibility.
- Wash/dry routine.
- Ethics/sustainability importance.
- Return policy and size consistency.
- Price per wear, not just sticker price.

### Shoes

- Use: walking, office, gym, running, bad weather.
- Foot shape, width, arch, known pain points.
- Cushion vs ground feel.
- Waterproofing vs breathability.
- Sole durability and resoling.
- Return policy after indoor try-on.

## Household and Furniture

- Room/use case and dimensions.
- Materials and cleanability.
- Assembly and repairability.
- Weight, stability, child/pet constraints.
- Warranty and spare parts.
- Delivery/return friction.
- Long-term style fit vs trend.
